# Legal, Ethical & Human-Rights Governance Framework

## 1. Epistemic Guardrails
- Generated programs are strictly labeled `PROPOSED_RESEARCH_PROGRAM` with `verification_status = PROPOSED_UNVALIDATED`.
- No generated program may be represented as a deployed system, binding law, or legally validated instrument.

## 2. Human-Rights Risk Classification (4 Tiers)
- **Tier 4 — PROHIBITED_OR_MORATORIUM_CANDIDATE**: Uncorroborated biometric mass surveillance, automated criminal adjudication without human judge, or social-scoring-style profiling.
- **Tier 3 — HIGH_RISK**: Pretrial/sentencing risk scoring, predictive policing, live facial recognition, or automated evidence admissibility filtering. Requires mandatory Human-in-the-Loop (HITL), algorithmic impact assessment (AIA), source-code/auditability, and right to contest.
- **Tier 2 — MODERATE_RISK**: Prosecutorial/judicial research assistants, multilingual ASR transcription, and case triage. Requires citation verification and human sign-off.
- **Tier 1 — LOW_RISK**: Anonymisation, court scheduling, public statute indexing, and administrative backlog forecasting.

## 3. Quality Scoring Rubric (100-Point Scale)
- Epistemic & Source Grounding: 25 pts
- Institutional & Legal System Fit: 20 pts
- Human-Rights Safeguard Completeness: 25 pts
- Technical Feasibility (Zero-Budget / Open Stack): 15 pts
- Originality & Non-Redundancy: 15 pts
