## 1. Architectural Thesis
The **IJC Global AI & Justice Innovation Universe** demonstrates that a solo researcher on standard CPU hardware can build, validate, and publish a globally comparative AI-and-justice research ecosystem with **zero incremental cash expenditure**. Instead of materializing one trillion redundant text rows, the system models a **12-dimensional combinatorial design space** (`8,957,952,000,000` addressable configurations) linked to **30 global research pillars** and grounded in empirical comparative evidence.

## 2. Empirical Grounding & Epistemic Separation
All real-world evidence is strictly separated from proposed research specifications:
- **Empirical Layer (`EXISTING_REAL_WORLD_RECORD`)**: Audited all 47 [Oxford AI Justice Atlas](https://www.techandjustice.bsg.ox.ac.uk/ai-atlas) URLs (46 `VERIFIED_ACCESSIBLE`; `OXF-AIATLAS-44` Zambia honestly logged as `SOURCE_INACCESSIBLE` with zero-imputation), yielding a 46-country Comparative Atlas, Governance Readiness Index (`mean=50.22/100`), **350** documented real-world systems, and **238** verified case/statutory authorities.
- **Generative Layer (`PROPOSED_RESEARCH_PROGRAM` / `PROPOSED_UNVALIDATED`)**: Deterministically generated, JSON-Schema-validated, and deduplicated portfolios at `100`, `1,000`, `10,000`, `100,000` (`1.37s`), and `1,000,000` (`5.84s`, `171,232.9` prog/sec) scales, plus **Top-10** Pilot Prototypes and AIA Dossiers.
- **Experimental Prototypes (`PROTOTYPE_EXPERIMENTAL_NONBINDING`)**: Four offline CPU prototypes—Grounded Citation Verification Gate (`6/6`), Judgment Anonymisation Gate (`4/4`), Risk-Scoring Disparity Simulator (`0.2386` FPR gap detection), and Synthetic Media Chain-of-Custody Gate (`4/4`)—alongside a 50-term 5-language glossary (`en`, `id_ms`, `es`, `fr`, `ar`) and a zero-CDN GitHub Pages portal (`docs/index.html`).
