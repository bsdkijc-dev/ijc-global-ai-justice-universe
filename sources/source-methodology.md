# Source Methodology — IJC Global AI & Justice Innovation Universe

## 1. Epistemic Grounding & Classification
Every record in the IJC Global AI & Justice Innovation Universe must strictly separate empirical reality from normative or speculative design:

- `EXISTING_REAL_WORLD_RECORD`: Documented AI applications, statutes, judicial policies, or court cases retrieved from verified external sources (such as the Oxford AI Justice Atlas).
- `PROPOSED_RESEARCH_PROGRAM`: Combinatorial program specifications generated by the 12-dimensional engine. **Never** described as deployed technology or legally validated.
- `UNVERIFIED_INACCESSIBLE_SOURCE`: Supplied URLs that could not be accessed or indexed during audit (e.g., `OXF-AIATLAS-44` Zambia). Retained in the access log with empty content fields (zero-imputation).

## 2. Data Hygiene & Zero-Imputation
- All CSV files use UTF-8 encoding.
- Missing metadata (e.g., missing `publication_or_update_date` or `reported_cases`) is left as an empty string `""` rather than guessed or backfilled.
- Original URLs are preserved verbatim.
- No personal identifiable information (PII) beyond public institutional/case names is stored.
